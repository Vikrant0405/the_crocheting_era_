FAQ

Questions before you order?

Answers to common customer and creator questions.

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes. Custom colours, combinations and selected designs can be discussed before placing an order.

Do you accept bulk flower orders?

Yes. Bulk crochet flowers are available for crochet artists, resellers and small businesses. Send quantity, colour/design requirements and required date for a quote.

How early should I place an order?

For custom and bulk orders, earlier is better. Production time depends on quantity, design and deadline.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Colour availability can vary by yarn stock, so confirm preferred shades before ordering.

How can I order?

Use the enquiry form or contact us through Instagram/WhatsApp. Include product, quantity, colour and required date.
